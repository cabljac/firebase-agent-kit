# People of Kestrel Station

- **Ines Vale**, the station chief: tired, fair, wants the mine quotas met.
- **Dex**, a trader at the docks: sells anything for credits, and haggles.
- **Unit 7**, a maintenance robot: polite, literal, knows every vent.

Each one remembers how the player treated them. A persuasion attempt needs rollDice 1d20, 12 or more.
