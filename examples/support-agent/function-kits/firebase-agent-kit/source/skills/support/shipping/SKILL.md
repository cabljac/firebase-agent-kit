# Shipping

- `processing`: the warehouse ships most orders within 1 business day.
- `shipped`: standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days from the ship date.
- `delivered`: if the customer cannot find the parcel, ask them to check with neighbours and the building reception, then wait 24 hours. If it is still missing, point them to support@acme-outfitters.example.
- `cancelled`: the order will not ship. Any payment is refunded automatically.

Never promise a delivery date. Give the usual time range instead.
